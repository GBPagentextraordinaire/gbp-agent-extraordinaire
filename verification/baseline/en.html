Frequently asked questions

How long does a typical engagement run?

Most engagements run 12 to 16 weeks, from discovery through to a working product in market.

We scope in two-week increments so you can stop or extend at any checkpoint.

Do you work with in-house teams?

Yes. We embed alongside your people rather than replacing them, and we hand over full documentation and code ownership at the end of every engagement.

What does the discovery sprint cost?

Discovery is a fixed fee, agreed before we start. There are no variable hours and no change requests during the sprint.

Which sectors do you work in?

We focus on financial services, healthcare, industrial manufacturing and logistics — sectors where regulation and legacy systems make new ventures hard to launch.

Can you support us after launch?

We offer a 12-month growth retainer covering product iteration, analytics and team coaching. It is optional and priced separately.
